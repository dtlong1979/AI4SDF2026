01 About the conference

A timely forum for responsible innovation

Hanoi Open University is pleased to announce the International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Data Science for a Sustainable Digital Future (AI4SDF 2026).

The conference brings together scholars, policymakers and industry experts to share interdisciplinary insights and discuss cutting-edge applications of AI and data science in promoting sustainability across education, healthcare, agriculture, environment and governance.

Its scope spans the most recent shifts in the field — agentic AI and multi-agent systems, new-generation large language models, multimodal and generative AI — read against the requirements of sustainable development, innovation and national digital transformation. Alongside the technology, AI4SDF 2026 gives equal weight to consequences: the labour market, higher education, and the ethics and governance of responsible AI.

The conference is also a standing opportunity to build and strengthen an annual network of cooperation among universities and research institutes in Vietnam and abroad, and to put forward concrete policy recommendations on responsible AI development, data security and the legal frameworks needed to keep AI deployment transparent and equitable.

02 Key themes

Five tracks, one question

How do we build AI and data systems that are powerful enough to matter and accountable enough to trust? Select a track to see its suggested topics.

T1 AI & Data Science in the Sustainable Digital Era

Emerging technologies; AI and the UN Sustainable Development Goals; global and Vietnamese case studies for sustainability.

  • Agentic AI and multi-agent systems
  • New-generation large language models and multimodal AI
  • From assistive tool to autonomous agent: AI's shifting role in society and the digital economy
  • Applied LLMs: retrieval-augmented generation, fine-tuning and production deployment
  • Vietnam in the global picture: opportunities and adoption pathways
T2 Applications Across the Pillars of Sustainability

Environmental, social and economic sustainability, including sustainable agriculture and green supply chains.

  • AI as innovation infrastructure: productivity, digital products and services
  • Smart agriculture, renewable energy, sustainable urban management, environmental monitoring
  • Green AI: energy cost and carbon footprint of large models; energy-efficient computation
  • Data-driven business models and digital transformation for SMEs
  • AI for healthcare, public services and inclusive development
T3 AI for Sciences & Predictive Analytics

Scientific machine learning and forecasting under real-world constraints.

  • Spatio-temporal analytics with graph neural networks and transformers
  • Green supply-chain forecasting
  • Generative AI for advanced materials
  • Scientific data pipelines, benchmarking and reproducibility
T4 Digital Twins & Smart IoT Systems

Intelligence at the edge, and the systems that keep it trustworthy.

  • Edge AI and TinyML
  • Zero-trust IoT and blockchain security
  • Smart-city and smart-grid digital twins
  • Sensing, simulation and closed-loop control for sustainable infrastructure
T5 Ethics, Challenges, and Responsible AI

Algorithmic bias, data privacy, social justice, AI and data science skills, and the legal and ethical frameworks for responsible deployment.

  • Cybersecurity in the age of AI: AI as both defensive and offensive instrument
  • National data sovereignty: data governance, digital infrastructure and data-residency policy
  • Responsible AI governance: transparency, traceability and the protection of civic rights
  • AI and the labour market: jobs displaced, jobs created, jobs transformed
  • Future skills and the university's role: curriculum reform, AI-integrated teaching, smart governance
  • Academic integrity and intellectual property in the era of generative AI

Submissions that cut across tracks are welcome — indicate your preferred track at submission and the programme committee will route the paper accordingly.

University of the Philippines Open University

Associate Professor working on information systems, data science and institutional digital transformation, with a PhD in Engineering (Computer Science) from Keio University. She serves as Assistant to the Chancellor for Institutional Analytics and Insights and as Data Protection Officer at UPOU, and chairs the Organizing Committee of the ICDE World Conference 2027. Prof. Dr. Nantha Kumar Subramaniam

Dean, Faculty of Computing and Analytics — Open University Malaysia

Professor and Dean at Open University Malaysia, and formerly Adviser on Technology-enabled Learning at the Commonwealth of Learning in Canada.
