About the conference

Hanoi Open University is pleased to announce the International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Data Science for a Sustainable Digital Future (AI4SDF 2026).

The conference aims to bring together scholars, policymakers and industry experts to share interdisciplinary insights and discuss cutting-edge applications of AI and data science in promoting sustainability across education, healthcare, agriculture, environment and governance.

It provides a timely forum to explore the implications of AI and data science, promote responsible innovation, and propose solutions for a sustainable digital future.

Key themes and suggested topics

T1

AI & Data Science in the Sustainable Digital Era

Emerging technologies; AI and the UN SDGs; global and Vietnamese case studies for sustainability.

T2

Applications Across the Pillars of Sustainability

Environmental, social and economic sustainability, including sustainable agriculture and green supply chains.

T3

AI for Sciences & Predictive Analytics

Spatio-temporal analytics with GNNs and transformers; green supply-chain forecasting; generative AI for advanced materials.

T4

Digital Twins & Smart IoT Systems

Edge AI and TinyML; zero-trust IoT and blockchain security; smart-city and smart-grid twins.

T5

Ethics, Challenges, and Responsible AI

Algorithmic bias; data privacy; social justice; AI and data science skills; legal and ethical frameworks for responsible deployment.

Scope

Original research · Interdisciplinary perspectives · Responsible innovation

Publication opportunities

  • Conference proceedings: all accepted and registered papers will be published in the conference proceedings.
  • DOI: each paper will be assigned a digital object identifier from CrossRef.
  • Indexing: the proceedings shall be submitted to Google Scholar for indexing.
  • ISBN: the conference proceedings will be published with an ISBN number in soft form.
  • Selected papers: the best articles will be submitted for publication in the Journal of Computer Science and Cybernetics.
